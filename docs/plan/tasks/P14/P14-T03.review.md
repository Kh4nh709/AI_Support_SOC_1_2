  - Why it matters: once an alert is decided, the definite verdict and the reasons are invisible everywhere in the UI, even though the API serves them. The report presents this as its own interpretation; nobody ruled it.
  - Fix: carry `proposed`/`heldBy` in the `decided` state and render the `Conclusion` line (or at least the held reasons) in the "① đề xuất" box. Keep the Khớp row computed from the stored verdict.
