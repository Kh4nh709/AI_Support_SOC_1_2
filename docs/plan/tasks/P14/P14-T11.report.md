## Deviations
- `evaluate_case`'s `CaseEvaluation` has more fields than the brief's list (the job writes its row from them).
- `rag.lookup` has no `before` parameter and `rag/` is read-only: `tier2/investigate.py` imports its two private validators `_history_of`, `_matches_of`, so one place checks what reaches a prompt.
- Accuracy and recall are over the subjects the model answered (`answered`); the rest are in `no_answer` by outcome (n is shown). Agreement in `calibrate` is of the alerts covered at t (a share of all alerts would only fall); the covered/agree counts are in the table.
- A `budget` lookup is reported on its own line; an evaluation that raises something unknown becomes an `error` outcome (class on stderr) instead of losing the run.
