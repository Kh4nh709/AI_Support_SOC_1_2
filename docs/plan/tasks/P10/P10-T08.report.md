## 1. Built

All paths are under `frontend/src/`.

**`api/client.ts`.** `ApiError(status, detail, body)`; `setTokenSource(get, onUnauthorized)`; `api<T>(path, init)` with the base
`/api`. It sends `Authorization: Bearer <token>` when the token source has one, `content-type: application/json` and
`JSON.stringify` for `init.json`, keeps the caller's own headers, answers `null` for a 204, and throws `ApiError` for every
non-2xx. `detail` is the server's `detail` (the 409 conflict body and the 422 list arrive intact) or, with none, the whole
body; `body` is always the whole parsed body (a non-JSON error body gives `null`). On a 401 it calls the `onUnauthorized` hook
**only when the request carried a token and that token is still the current one**, then throws `ApiError(401)`.

**`api/types.ts`.** `ROLES`, `Role`, `isRole`, `LoginResponse`, `LockedBody`, `MeResponse` (the shapes of
`backend/app/web/routers/auth.py`: login gives `{token, role, user_id, expires_at}` and no username; `/me` gives the username).

**`api/auth.ts`.** `postLogin(username, password)`, `fetchMe()`, `postLogout()` over `api`.

**`auth/session.ts`.** `Session = {token, role, username, expiresAt}`.
- `loadSession()` reads `sessionStorage["soc.session"]`, checks the shape and the expiry, and drops a bad or expired entry.
- `saveSession(s)` writes `sessionStorage` and nothing else (a refused store is swallowed: the session still lives in memory).
- `forgetSession()` empties this tab's copy and says nothing to the others; `clearSession()` does that and broadcasts
  `{type: "logout"}` on `BroadcastChannel("soc-session")`.
- `startSessionSharing(onLogout)` answers another tab's `{type: "session-request"}` with `{type: "session", session}` and calls
  `onLogout` on a `logout` message; it never rebroadcasts a logout (that would ping-pong). It ignores anything that is not a
  message of the protocol, and returns its own stop function.
- `requestSessionFromOtherTabs(timeoutMs = 300, signal?)` asks, waits for one valid unexpired `session` answer or the timeout, and
  gives `null` on no answer or an abort.

**`auth/AuthProvider.tsx`.** `AuthState = {session, ready, signedOut, login, logout}`, `AuthContext`, `useAuth()`,
`AuthProvider`. The session is held in a ref (what the client reads) and in state (what renders). Mount, in this order:
1. `loadSession()`; if there is none, `requestSessionFromOtherTabs(300)` and adopt (and save) the answer only if nothing else set
   a session meanwhile;
2. `GET /api/auth/me` to confirm: a 401 ends the session (no broadcast), any other failure keeps the session, and the role and
   username of `/me` replace the stored ones when the token is still the same and the role is one we know;
3. `ready` becomes true. Until then guards wait (no redirect to `/login` during a reload).

A timer clears the session at `expiresAt` (capped at `2**31 - 1` ms and re-armed if it fires early). A `logout` broadcast from
another tab ends this session locally. The client's 401 hook ends the session locally too: **it never broadcasts**, so a wrong
password at the login form of one tab cannot sign out the others. `login(u, p)` posts, validates the answer, saves, applies,
and navigates (`replace`) to `landingAfterLogin(location.state, role)`. `logout()` sets `signedOut`, posts `/auth/logout`
(a failure is ignored), `clearSession()` (broadcast), and navigates to `/login`. All of it runs under `StrictMode` in the tests.

**`auth/redirect.ts`.** `roleHome(role)` (tier1 `/queue`, tier2 and admin `/cases`), `pathOf(location)`, and
`landingAfterLogin(state, role)`: a `from` in the router state is honoured only when it is a local path (starts with a single
`/`, not `//` or `/\`, and not the login page itself), otherwise the role's home.

**`auth/RequireRole.tsx`.** `RequireRole({roles, children})`: not ready renders `<p role="status">` "Đang tải…"; no session goes
to `/login` carrying `{from: <path + query>}` (or nothing, after an explicit Đăng xuất); a role not in `roles` goes to `/queue`
carrying `{forbidden: true}`. `ForbiddenNotice` renders `<p role="alert">{S.forbidden}</p>` when the location state says so.

**`pages/LoginPage.tsx`.** A form (username with `autocomplete="username"` and autofocus; password `type="password"`
`autocomplete="current-password"`). 401 shows `S.login.invalid`; 423 shows `S.login.locked(HH:MM)` with the lock time from
`locked_until` in +07; 503 shows `S.login.disabled`; anything else (a network error included) shows `S.common.unknownError`, all
in `role="alert"`. A second submit while one is in flight is dropped, and the button is disabled meanwhile. The password lives in
component state only. A visitor who is already signed in is redirected to their landing page.

**`components/AppHeader.tsx`.** `AppHeader({live?})`: the app name always; when signed in, `<nav aria-label="Điều hướng chính">` with
Hàng đợi (`/queue`), Case (`/cases`, tier2 and admin) and, for admin only, "Quản trị (UI cũ)" as
`<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="http://<window.location.hostname>:8000/admin/rules">`; then the `live` slot
(P10-T09 fills it), the username, the role chip and Đăng xuất. The current page carries `aria-current="page"`.

**`routes.tsx`, `app.tsx`.** `AppRoutes`: a `Shell` (header + one `<main>` with the forbidden notice and the outlet) around
`/login`, `/` (redirects to the role home), `/queue` and `/alerts/:alertId` (all roles), `/cases` and `/cases/:caseId`
(`RequireRole` tier2, admin), and `*` (redirects to `/`). The pages T09 to T11 will fill are placeholders with an `<h1>`.
`App` is `BrowserRouter > AuthProvider > AppRoutes`. The placeholder of P10-T07 is replaced; its semantics (banner with the app

## 4. Deviations

   the same words the brief asks for.
2. **`ApiError` has explicit fields, not parameter properties**: `erasableSyntaxOnly` forbids the latter. It also has a third
   field, `body` (the whole parsed body), because the brief's `detail ?? data` would lose the 423 `locked_until` of the flat
   `{detail: "locked", locked_until}` body.
3. **The 401 hook fires only when the request carried a token and it is still the current one.** The brief says a 401 calls the
   hook; a wrong password at the login form has no token and must not sign out the other tabs, and a slow request of an old
   token must not sign out a newer login.

   `/login` during the restore and the `/me` check; `signedOut` says the sign-out was deliberate, so the next login lands on the
   role home. The router (react-router 8, `useTransitions` at its default) applies location updates as transitions and the
   provider's state as urgent updates, so this ordering cannot be left to batching.
5. **A `from` return path** (after a deep link or an involuntary session loss) is kept and honoured, local paths only. The brief
   says "navigates to the role home"; that is what happens whenever there is no `from`.
6. **Additions to `session.ts`**: `forgetSession` and `startSessionSharing` (so the provider can end a session without
   broadcasting, and answer another tab), and an `AbortSignal` parameter on `requestSessionFromOtherTabs` (StrictMode runs the

7. **Files beyond the brief's list**: `auth/redirect.ts`, `formatClock` in `format.ts`, `S.forbidden`, `S.nav.label` and `S.role`

### 7.1 Logout on a stalled backend — `954848f`

`logout()` in `auth/AuthProvider.tsx` used to `await postLogout()` before it cleared anything, so a backend that never answered
left the user on a page they believed they had signed out of. Now it keeps the current token in a local variable, sets
`signedOut`, then `clearSession()` (storage and the `logout` broadcast), `apply(null)` and `navigate("/login")`, and only then
sends `POST /api/auth/logout` with the old token, an `AbortController` timeout of 5000 ms (`LOGOUT_TIMEOUT_MS`), and every error
ignored. `postLogout(token, signal?)` in `api/auth.ts` takes the token and sends it as an explicit `Authorization` header: the
client's own token source is empty by then, and it is read synchronously at the call, after `apply(null)`. With no token (a
second press of Đăng xuất) nothing is posted.

- Tests (`AuthProvider.test.tsx`):
  - "logout does not wait for a stalled backend: it ends here at once, and the POST still carries the old token": the logout
    handler is `await delay("infinite")`; within 500 ms the state is `ready none`, the storage is empty, the page is `/login`,
    another tab has heard `{type: "logout"}`, and the handler saw `Bearer tok-1`.
  - "gives up on the logout POST after about five seconds": spies `setTimeout`, finds the 5000 ms timer, runs it, and the
    handler's `request.signal` reports the abort.
  - the two older logout tests (Bearer, broadcast, `/login`; a failing request) stay green.
- Command: `npm test -- --run src/auth/AuthProvider.test.tsx`.
- Red step: against the unchanged `logout()` (the old order, the tests written first) —
  `× logout does not wait for a stalled backend …` and `× gives up on the logout POST after about five seconds`, both
  `AssertionError: expected 'ready stale/tier1/tok-1' to be 'ready none'` (2 failed, 15 passed). After the change: 17 passed.

### 7.2 The return path — `f035810`

`isLocalPath` in `auth/redirect.ts` now refuses, in this order: not a string or no leading `/`; a leading `//`; **any backslash**;
**any control character** (U+0000 to U+001F and U+007F, a `charCodeAt` scan because `no-control-regex` forbids the range in a
regex); the login page itself. Then `new URL(from, window.location.origin)` has the last word: another origin, or a scheme other
than `http:` and `https:`, is refused, and so is anything the parser throws on. A refused `from` lands on the role home.

- Tests (new `auth/redirect.test.ts`, 28): accepted, and returned as written: `/queue?band=high`, `/alerts/12`,
  `/cases/3?tab=notes#top`, `/queue?next=//not-a-host`. Refused, landing on `/queue` for tier1 and `/cases` for tier2:

  `https://evil.example`, `http://evil.example/queue`, a `data:` URL, `queue`, `/login`, `/login?x=1`, an empty string, and
  a number, an object and `null`. Then the control characters and backslashes that stay inside the origin for a parser, and so
  are refused only by the character rules: `/qu\teue`, `/qu\neue`, `/queue\u0000x`, `/queue\u001fx`, `/queue\u007fx`,
  `/queue\..\cases`, `/queue\x`.
- Command: `npm test -- --run src/auth/redirect.test.ts`.
- Red steps:
  - against the old `isLocalPath`, tests first: 9 failed (`/\t/…`, `/\n/…`, `/\r/…`, the five in-path control characters,
    the in-path backslash), 19 passed.
  - the control-character check dropped: 5 failed, the five in-path control cases (`× refuses a tab inside the path (a control
    character)` … `a DEL inside the path`); 23 passed.
  - the backslash rule dropped: 1 failed, `× refuses a backslash anywhere in the path`.
