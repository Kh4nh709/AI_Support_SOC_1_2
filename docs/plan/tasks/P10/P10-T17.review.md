### `24ac3df` — the escalate race (DEC-166 item 3)

`transitions.escalate`'s ③a `UPDATE` now also requires:

```sql
alert_id = <trigger> OR status = 'queued_tier1'
  OR (status = 'tier1_active' AND acknowledged_by = <actor>)
```

- This is the same predicate as the sweep's `WHERE` in `correlation.cluster_ids_query`:

  ```sql
  status IN ('queued_tier1','tier1_active')
  AND NOT (status = 'tier1_active' AND acknowledged_by IS DISTINCT FROM actor)
  ```

  So a sibling the SELECT legitimately returned cannot fail the UPDATE without a concurrent change. A `tier1_active` row with a NULL holder is excluded by both.
- The trigger is exempt, which keeps the API's "escalate an alert you were given" behaviour unchanged.
- A mismatch raises `StaleState` through the existing rowcount check. `decide.escalate`'s savepoint undoes the case, and the route answers 409 `already_in_case`.
- The test uses two real connections on committed data. Its red step, with the clause dropped, is recorded in the report: "DID NOT RAISE".
