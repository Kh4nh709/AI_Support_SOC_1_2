## 1. Built

All paths are under `frontend/src/`.

**`api/tier1.ts`** (additive to T09's queue part). `AlertView` completed from `queue.get_alert_view(mode="analyst")`; the
keys the server may strip (`suggestion`, the ① meta) are optional, and `requested_alert_id` is a string. `Suggestion` carries
the verdict, reasons (quote and source), gate steps, facts, verifier, `correlated_at_analysis`. `fetchAlertView(id, signal)`,
`postAcknowledge`, `postDecide({decision, reason, seen_occurrence_count})`, `postEscalate({title, severity?})` (201),
`postReopen({reason})`, `DecisionResult`, and `ConflictBody` (a 409 body is flat: `kind`, the two counts, the two statuses).

**`features/tier1/AlertPage.tsx`.** `/alerts/:alertId`.
- **Read.** `useQuery({queryKey: ["alert", alertId]})`. `alerts.changed` for the id (or `many`) refetches it through
  `LiveProvider`'s throttle; with no socket it asks every 30 s (`OFFLINE_POLL_MS`, from the queue page); while the server's word
  for ① is `pending` it asks every 10 s. A `key={alertId}` on the screen makes a new id a new page.
- **A duplicate** answers with its head (`alert_id` is the head's; `requested_alert_id` is the id asked for). The changes the
  socket names are the head's, so the head is read under its own key as well (`["alert", head]`, seeded with the view that named
  it, fresh for 5 s so it costs no request), and the newer of the two answers is shown. The page says
  `Bản sao của <head id>` and links the head.
- 404 → `PageError` "not found" (no retry); any other failure → `PageError` with "Thử lại"; loading is `role="status"`.
- Layout: breadcrumb (queue link, the head when a duplicate, the id), an `h1` (description, else the id), then a grid
  `lg:grid-cols-[minmax(0,1fr)_520px]`: the left column is a labelled `<section>` (h2 "Thông tin từ Wazuh", a hint) with the
  blocks of `AlertSections` and the decision panel at its foot; the right column is `AiColumn`. The page renders no `<main>`
  (the shell has it).

**`features/tier1/AlertSections.tsx`.** `Summary` (band badge: the server's `risk_band`, else the severity label; category;
status label; a `KeyValueGrid` of rule, level, agent, occurrences, first and last seen, who acknowledged and when, when and why
closed, and the link `Xem case #N` to `/cases/<id>`), `EventDetails` (srcip, dstip, ports, user, decoder, rule groups, MITRE,
source, origin host, alert and event time; none of them, no block), `RawLog`, `Accounts`, `Enrichment` (asset, identity, IOC
and the lookup statuses; what a lookup has not found is "không rõ"), `Correlation` (the clusters of the ±2 h window in a
`DataTable`, the samples as links to `/alerts/<id>`, and "gợi ý dựa trên N cụm … hiện có M" is in the ① column) and `Playbook`
(text in a `<p className="whitespace-pre-wrap">`, "no playbook" when the category has none). Every block shows only when the
view carries its data. Times are `formatTime`/`formatDateTime` (Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh).

**`features/tier1/AiColumn.tsx`.** The state is `aiStateOfView(view)` (T09) and nothing else: the column never infers
`ran`, `pending` or `failed` from another field, from the time, or from the socket. `AiColumn` takes an optional `state` only
for tests.
- **ready:** the verdict (label of the action, confidence, `⚑` with an accessible name when the gate forced it), the reasons
  with their quote and source, the gate steps (original suggestion → after the gate), the verifier, the facts used, the
  correlation note, and the meta line the API carries (when it was analysed). An unknown action shows as its raw code.
- **hidden (blind arm, DEC-101):** `S.ai1.hidden` and its explanation, and nothing of ①. The branch never reads
  `view.suggestion`.
- **pending:** "① đang phân tích"; **unavailable:** "Không có gợi ý ①" and `stopped_by` with its label (raw when unknown);
- **decided:** the decision beside what ① said (`decidedHeading`, who decided, and ①'s action and confidence, or "none").

**`features/tier1/DecisionPanel.tsx`** (+ `DecisionForms.tsx`, `conflict.ts`, `draft.ts`, `useMe.ts`). Props
`{view, me, onDone?}`; it renders nothing unless `me.role === "tier1"` (tier 2 and admin read the page).
- `queued_tier1` → only "Nhận xử lý". `tier1_active` → who holds it comes from `/api/auth/me` (`useMe`: `Session` has no user
  id): held by me → decide (false positive / benign, a reason, the count shown) and escalate (title, severity); held by
  someone else → read-only with `acknowledged_by_name`; while `/me` loads, or after it failed (with "Thử lại"), the panel never
  guesses. `auto_closed` → the reopen form. Any other decided status → "decided as …" with the close reason, no action. An
  unknown status → `notActionable` with its raw code.
- **One `useMutation`** with a lock (a `useRef` and a `busy` state): every button is disabled in flight, a double click posts
  once. On success: the draft is removed (decide and escalate), a `Toast` names the fan-out count (decide) or links the new case
  (escalate), `onDone(result)` is called, and `["queue"]`, `["alert", head]` and `["alert", requested id]` are invalidated.
- **Decide sends the count on screen** (`seen_occurrence_count = view.occurrence_count`), never a fresh read.
- **Errors:** 401 → "phiên hết hạn" (the typed reason stays; the client's 401 hook ends the session); 409 → the server's own
  message with both counts (`clusterGrew(seen, now, …)`, `alreadyDecided`, `alreadyInCase`, `notReopenable`, else `notActive`)
  and the view is refetched, so the form shows the new count; 422 → the message at the reason (or the title) field with the text
  kept; 503 → `S.decision.timeout`; anything else → `unknownError`.
- **The reason draft (Review Focus 4).** `sessionStorage["soc.draft.<alert_id>"]` = JSON `{user_id, text}`, written as the
  reason is typed (removed when the box is emptied), restored once when the form is first drawn and only when `user_id` is the
  signed-in user's (`sameUser`: compared as text, case-insensitive), removed after a successful decide. `auth/session.ts` was
  read: logout and forget remove only `soc.session`, so a draft survives a re-login. Nothing is ever stored in `localStorage`.

**`components/`** `KeyValueGrid` (label/value pairs, empty values dropped, `visibleItems`), `LogBlock` (one
`<pre className="max-h-96 overflow-auto whitespace-pre-wrap">{text}</pre>`, a copy button with

### 6.2 R19: the decide form holds the count it was drawn with

Before: the form sent `view.occurrence_count`, and the page refetches the view on `alerts.changed`. If the count went from 37
to 120 while the analyst typed, the POST sent 120, the server saw a delta of 0 and never answered `cluster_grew`, and the
fan-out closed 120 alerts without the review the guard exists for (`S.decision.seenHint` promised the opposite).

Now (`DecisionForms.tsx` `DecideForm`, and `DecisionPanel.tsx` for the 409): the count the form sends is held in the form's own
state (`seen`). It is taken from the view when the form is first drawn (or, if the view had no count yet, from the first one
that arrives), and it changes on exactly one event: a 409 `cluster_grew` answer to a decide. The panel then refetches the
view (`refresh()`), and passes the form `adopted = { count }`, the count of the view just fetched (the one the 409 message
shows the analyst; the 409's own `occurrence_count` only when no view is in the cache). The form takes it into `seen` on the
render that sees a new `adopted`; the next submit sends it. A live refetch does not move `seen`. While the view's count
differs from `seen` the form shows `S.decision.seenChanged(seen, now)` ("37 → 120: cụm đã tăng khi bạn đọc — khi bạn gửi, hệ
thống sẽ yêu cầu xem lại", `aria-live="polite"`) beside `S.decision.seenHint(seen)`; nothing is adopted silently, and a
second growth after the 409 warns again. Every other 409 kind, and 422/503, leave `seen` alone.

- Tests: `DecisionPanel.test` describe "the count the decision is made on (ruling R19)" (5): "keeps sending the count the form
  was drawn with when a live refetch brings a larger one" (37 then 120: the notice shows, the reason typed stays, the POST
  carries 37); "holds the new count only when the server answers cluster_grew, and sends it on the next submit" (POSTs
  `[37, 120]`); "warns again when the cluster grows once more after the 409, and still does not adopt it silently" (`[37,
  120]`, with 150 only noticed); "does not adopt a count for a 409 that is not cluster_grew" (`[37, 37]`); "says nothing while
  the count on the server is the count the form holds". `AlertPage.test` "sends the count read when the form was drawn, then
