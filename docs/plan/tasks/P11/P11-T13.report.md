### Per state

One read, `GET /api/admin/llm/usage?range=` (key `adminKeys.llmUsage(range)`), refetched every 60 s (`refetchInterval`, which TanStack
pauses while the tab is hidden), `placeholderData: keepPreviousData`, so a range switch keeps the page and the form's edits.

- **Normal.** `h1`, the range select ("Khoảng thời gian": Tháng này, 24 giờ qua, 7 ngày qua, 30 ngày qua; the month first, the route's four),
  four tiles, the table by pipeline and role beside the limits block, the quota beside the last 20 calls.
- **Cost tile.** `$spent / $cap`, a `<progress>` (named; no `style`, the CSP forbids one) with the value held at the cap, "còn $X ·
  dự báo tuyến tính cuối tháng $Y" with `projected_usd` shown as the route gave it (floored at one day on the server, never recomputed),
  and the note that the month is the calendar month whatever the range says and starts at the database session's midnight.
  The state is `costState(month)`:
  - **red / stopped** = `spent_usd >= cap_usd`, never `pct` (the route floors `pct`): the canvas's alert text, the bar red at the cap;
  - **amber / warn** = `pct >= 80` and below the cap: "82% · sắp chạm trần. Khi chạm trần, …", no alert role;
  - **ok** otherwise. With no cap (limits unreadable): `$spent / —`, no bar, no notice, "Không có trần để so sánh".
  The tile carries `data-cost="ok|warn|stopped"`.
- **Calls, tokens, stopped tiles.** Calls: the total and "① n · ② n" (no chat: spec decision 5). Tokens: in / out ("2,44 tr / 0,41 tr"
  from 100,000 up) and the average input of each pipeline. Stopped: Σ `stopped` counts over Σ calls, with the reason most runs stopped on.
- **By pipeline and role.** `DataTable`: pipeline and role (① triage · proposer / verifier, ② điều tra case, anything else as it came),
  calls, with result, "Dừng vì" (each `stopped_by` worded, largest first; the server's `other` bucket is "lý do khác"; an unknown code is
  shown as text), p50 / p95, cost. The sentence "Tool call: 0 — mô hình không gọi công cụ nào." stays under it (decision 4).
- **No calls.** In place of the table: "Chưa có lượt gọi model nào trong {24 giờ qua | 7 ngày qua | 30 ngày qua | tháng này}" and the
  hint to take a longer range. The tiles read zero, the form is still there, and `recent` (the 20 newest, whatever the range) is still shown.
- **Quota.** "Dùng hạn mức ② · 24 giờ gần nhất", the limit, each user's runs and what is left; a user with `remaining 0` reads
  "0 · hết lượt" in red (also when over a lowered limit); an unreadable limit and `remaining null` are dashes; nobody is "Chưa ai chạy ②…".
  The note says the count is the last 24 hours, not the calendar day; no text says "midnight" (a test greps the panel).
- **Recent calls.** Time (`formatTime`), pipeline and role, subject, tokens in / out, latency, outcome, cost. The subject links to
  `/alerts/:id` or `/cases/:id` (id encoded; the text is "alert ….1662100", the whole id in `title`); another subject type or no id is
  text or a dash. Outcome: ① proposer's `suggested_action` and ②'s `suggested_conclusion` through the words the UI already has
  (`S.ai1.action`, `S.ai2.suggestedConclusion`, unknown shown as it came); a ① **verifier** row says "kết luận riêng: …", its own
  `structured_only_verdict`, never an agreement with the proposer; `stopped:<code>` reads "dừng · …".
- **Limits block (`LlmLimits.tsx`).**
  - Three number fields in the canvas's order and wording. Each field's `min`/`max` attributes and the "Từ a đến b" hint are
    `limits.bounds[field]` (no 600 or any limit in the code). Its source line is `limits.sources[LIMIT_KEY_OF_FIELD[field]]`: "từ .env",
    or "sửa trên web bởi X lúc dd/MM/yyyy hh:mm:ss" (`formatDateTime`; the name is left out when the user is gone). `clamped` adds a note on that field.
  - "Lưu hạn mức" is disabled until a field differs. A blank, a decimal or a value outside the bounds is shown at the field before anything is
    sent (the route's own words, `aria-invalid`, `aria-describedby`, focus on the first); the server's 422 `{field, reason}`
    (`out_of_range` with its min and max, `not_integer`, `unknown`, `empty`) is shown at the field too, the dialog closes and the field
    takes the focus; after an `out_of_range` the page is read again so the bounds on screen are the server's.
  - The confirmation ("Lưu hạn mức mới?") lists the changed fields only, old → new (`$30 → $40`, `120 s → 90 s`). When the new cap is not
    above this month's spend it also shows the amber alert and the confirm becomes the red "Vẫn lưu". Huỷ and Esc send nothing and keep
    what was typed.
  - On success the dialog closes, the toast "Đã lưu hạn mức. Có hiệu lực ngay." shows, the PUT's `{limits}` is written into every cached
    usage query at once (so the form shows what the server holds, not what was typed), then `await invalidateAfter(client, "putLlmLimits")`.
  - In the dialog, which stays open: 503 `limits_unavailable` (and the page is read again, so the fail-closed panel can take over),
    403 `forbidden`, any other reason's text, "mất kết nối" for no answer, "máy chủ trả lỗi" for the rest. The confirm can be pressed again.
- **Fail closed.** `data.limits === null || data.limits_error !== null`: the block is a read-only panel ("Không đọc được hạn mức đã lưu;
  model đang dừng gọi … chứ không phải được gọi mà không có trần"), no field, no button, no dialog. The other blocks still render (the
  block failing alone).
- **Loading.** The page's own `h1` and a skeleton (`role="status"`, `aria-busy`, "Đang tải…").
- **Page error.** `PageError` ("Không tải được trang", Thử lại, link back) and, under it, the hint: the reason's text for a 503
  `timeout` / `query_failed` or a 403, "Máy chủ trả lỗi {status}", or "Mất kết nối". A later read that fails keeps the page and shows a
  "Không làm mới được dữ liệu" alert with a retry.

## 4. Deviations

From the card, the spec, the plan or the canvas, and why.

1. **Commit trailers.** The card says `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5`; the session's attribution instruction for this run says
   `Claude Sonnet 5.5`, which is the model that wrote the commits (P11-T10 did the same). Each message ends with that line and the `Claude-Session` line.
2. **The month note is added.** The canvas has the tile title "Chi phí tháng này" and the select "Tháng này", but no note. The card
   says to say it "if the canvas has a 'tháng này' note", and the plan row lists "the month starts at the database session's midnight". The tile
   shows one short note (the whole calendar month whatever the range; it starts at 0 h on the 1st in the database session's time zone),
   because otherwise "Chi phí tháng này" beside a "24 giờ qua" select reads as a contradiction.
3. **Canvas elements left out, with the reason.**
   - The "Model deepseek-v4-flash / Thinking tắt" lines of the limits block: the usage route sends neither, and a hard-coded model name would drift.
   - "Xem tất cả" (the canvas's link points at the same page).
   - The "chat 0" in the calls tile and the "Hỏi AI (chat)" row: spec decision 5 (no chat pipeline in v1.1).
4. **Canvas wording replaced where it was not true.**
   - Tool-call sentence: decision 4's "mô hình không gọi công cụ nào", not "một lần mỗi lượt" (① makes up to four sends).
   - The quota note no longer says the exhausted user "thấy nút chạy ② bị khoá": `CasePage` does not lock the button, it answers 429 on the press.
     It says the user cannot run ② again until their oldest run is older than 24 hours.
5. **The warning also fires when the new cap equals the spend** ("không cao hơn chi phí tháng này"; the canvas says "thấp hơn"), because
   the adapter stops at `spent >= cap`, so a cap equal to the spend stops the model too.
6. **The stopped tile** counts every row (Σ `stopped` over Σ calls); the canvas's "122 / 855 lượt ①" is the proposer row alone.
7. **Toast.** The house `Toast` has no 5 s timer (by design, its header says why); the saved note stays until closed, and no separate floating toast was built.
8. **Client-side checks before the dialog.** A blank, a decimal or an out-of-range value never reaches the PUT: the page shows the route's own
   text at the field. The server's 422 is still mapped at the field (and refreshes the bounds). The reason: a blank or a word cannot be
   sent as a JSON integer, and a value outside `limits.bounds` would otherwise cost a confirm round trip.
9. **A field's 422 closes the dialog** (the canvas draws the field errors on the form, without a dialog) and moves the focus to the field.
10. **Range select has four options** (the canvas draws three): the route's `30d` is offered too. The range is page state, not in the URL (spec §3: only digest and audit keep theirs).
11. **Files.** The card names `LlmPage.tsx`; the limits block is its own file, `LlmLimits.tsx`, and the pure figures are `llmFigures.ts`, so the page
    stays readable and the figures have their own unit tests.
12. **Money and number text** (`formatUsd`, `formatTokens`, …) lives in `llmFigures.ts`, not `format.ts`: `format.ts` is the shared time module and three
    other page Coders work in parallel. Times go through `format.ts` (`formatTime`, `formatDateTime`) as the card says.
13. **`AdminLayout.test.tsx` (a T10 file) is changed in one place.** It rendered every page as a placeholder with no `QueryClient`, expecting "đang xây dựng";
    the built `LlmPage` cannot be that. `BUILT = ["LlmPage"]` and `PLACEHOLDERS = PAGES.filter(...)` are new, and the two `it.each(PAGES)` that render a page
    are now `it.each(PLACEHOLDERS)`. `PAGES` itself, and so the test that its seven titles equal `S.admin.title.*`, is untouched.
