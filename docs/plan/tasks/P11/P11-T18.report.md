### I-2: the password in a failure snapshot

The finding was right, and the cause is wider than the finding. **Before the fix**, with a scratch backend (`E2E_BACKEND_DIR`) whose
`POST /api/admin/users` answers 500, scenario 2 failed at the toast and the scan said `secret found in .../error-context.md`: the
target failed on a leaked password, not only on the red test. And a probe (a login that fails: `loginAs(page, {username: "nobody"}, ...)`,
deleted afterwards) showed the same for **the v1.0 login helpers**: `error-context.md` held the password even though `clearingOnFailure`
had emptied the field. The reason is in Playwright 1.63: a failing matcher (`expect(locator).toBeVisible()`, `expect(page).toHaveURL()`)
takes a page snapshot at the instant it fails and hangs it on the error it throws (`matcherResult.ariaSnapshot`), and the report's
`error-context.md` is made from that. Emptying the field afterwards cannot change a snapshot already taken.

The fix, in `frontend/e2e/loginFailure.ts`: `clearingOnFailure` still empties the field and rethrows the very same error (so
`src/e2eLoginFailure.test.ts`, which pins that identity, passes unchanged), and now also deletes `matcherResult.ariaSnapshot` from it. With no
early snapshot on the error, the report takes its own when the test ends, after the field was emptied. Then:

- `clearingPasswords(panel, step)` (adminFixtures) runs the whole create section of scenario 2 (both creates) under `clearingOnFailure`
  on the password field and on its repeat;
- the form's own `toHaveValue("")` on the password, which prints the received value, is gone: `expectPasswordsEmpty` compares the
  **length** of `inputValue()`, so a failure says "a password field still holds what was typed" and nothing else;
- after the refused duplicate (the form keeps what was typed after a refusal) `emptyPasswords` empties both fields explicitly;
- the other scenarios that type a secret do it through `loginAs` and `signIn` (scenarios 2, 3), now covered by the same fix; no other
  place types one (`fillSecret` is the only way in, and its failures name the field, not the value).

**Proof, after the fix**, the same red (create route answering 500): scenario 2 is red (`1 failed`, `expect(locator).toBeVisible() failed`
at the toast), `ui_e2e_scan: no secret in 4 file(s) (8 secret(s) of the run searched for)`, and the target fails on the red test alone: no
"the run left a secret behind". The failure snapshot shows `textbox "Mật khẩu tạm" [active]` and `textbox "Nhập lại mật khẩu"` with no
