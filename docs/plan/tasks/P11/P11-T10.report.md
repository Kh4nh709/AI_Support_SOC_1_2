### `features/admin/ConfirmDialog.tsx`

`ConfirmDialog(props: ConfirmDialogProps)`, mounted while open, unmounted to close (a usage example is in its header comment).

| prop | meaning |
|---|---|
| `title: string` | The heading; the dialog's accessible name. |
| `children?: ReactNode` | The body. |
| `confirmLabel: string` | Text of the confirm button. |

| `tone?: "danger" \| "primary"` | Default `primary`. Danger is red and opens with the focus on cancel; primary is blue and opens on confirm. |
| `busy?: boolean` | Disables both buttons; the confirm button shows `S.admin.common.working` with `aria-busy="true"`; Esc does nothing. |

children already has it) and back to the element that had it when the dialog mounted, if still on the page; Tab and Shift+Tab wrap
inside, also when the focus was lost to the page or every button is disabled; a click on the overlay does not cancel.

## 5. Deviations

1. **Writes send `{}` everywhere, `retryJob` and `reloadInventory` included.** The card says to do it for `undoLabel` and `retryJob`
   "unless the handler takes no body, which you check". I checked: `api_retry_job(conn, claims, job_id)` and `reload_inventory(conn, _)` take
   no body, and FastAPI ignores one. I sent `{}` anyway so that one rule covers every write, and a handler that later reads a body (as
   `unlock` and the labels undo do) cannot be broken by a call that forgot it. The test pins the wire text `{}` for `retryJob`.
2. **`putLlmLimits` answers `{limits: LimitsOut}`**, not the `{effective, source}` of spec §5.2: that is what the P11-T03 handler returns
   (`limits_out`: the three values, `sources` per key, `bounds` per field). `LlmUsage` follows the P11-T04 handler (`month`,
   `by_pipeline_role`, `quota`, `recent`, `limits`, `limits_error`, `tool_calls`).
3. **`AdminError` has two kinds beyond the card's three examples, `message` and `messages`**, and the plain texts of the older routes are
   read as reason codes. The rules routes answer `detail: "rule not found"` and `detail: <PermanentError text>`, the labels routes answer
   three plain texts and `"labeler must be the current user"`, require_role answers `"forbidden"`, and the inventory reload answers a
   list of file-error texts: with only `reason | field | other` a page could not show any of them. A list of texts is `messages`; FastAPI's own
   list (objects) stays `other`. `ADMIN_REASONS` therefore has 28 codes (27 when written; `reserved` was added in fix round 1): the 20 the routes write as `{reason}` or `{field, reason}`;
   `forbidden` (the plain 403); `rule_not_found`, `unknown_cluster`, `already_labelled` and `labeler_mismatch` (plain texts of the rules and
   labels routes); and `frozen` and `nothing_to_undo` (the undo route of P11-T08; `frozen` is also the labels route's plain "the gold set is frozen").
4. **`ConfirmDialog`: two extra optional props, and some choices the card leaves open.** `confirmDisabled` and `hideConfirm` (the canvas draws
   a refusal with only "Đóng", and a reset-password dialog cannot confirm before its fields are valid). While `busy` the cancel button is
   disabled too and the confirm label becomes `S.admin.common.working`. A click on the overlay does not cancel. An `autoFocus` field of the
   children keeps the focus the dialog would otherwise move. `title` is a `string`.
5. **`adminKeys`.** The card's `llmUsage` name is used (the plan says `llm`). `digest` takes `string | null`. Added the prefixes and
   `ADMIN_INVALIDATES` / `invalidateAfter` so that "which key each mutation invalidates" is code and a test, not only a comment.
6. **Extra constants** in `api/admin.ts` (`ADMIN_LIMITS`, `AUDIT_GROUPS`, `HARD_BLOCK_CODES`, `DIGEST_VERDICTS`, `LABEL_CHOICES`,
   `LIMIT_FIELDS`, `OVERVIEW_SECTIONS`, `LLM_RANGES`) that the page tasks would otherwise each write by hand. **Corrected in fix round 1 (m4):**
   this item first said each is pinned to the backend or to its type by a test. Only four were (`ADMIN_LIMITS`, `AUDIT_GROUPS`,
   `HARD_BLOCK_CODES`, `DIGEST_VERDICTS`); `OVERVIEW_SECTIONS`, `LIMIT_FIELDS`, `ADMIN_RANGES`, `LLM_RANGES`, `LABEL_CHOICES` and
   `LABEL_CONFIDENCES` were not. Fix round 1 pins them, with the new `LIMIT_KEYS` and `LIMIT_KEY_OF_FIELD`. `S.admin.range` and
   `S.admin.bounds` are also extra.
7. **`LabelsNext.frozen` and `candidates_present` were typed required and the P11-T06 fields optional** (the base `1478196` had neither T06
   nor T08). **Corrected in fix round 1 (I1):** at `47bf6ac` both T06 and T08 are merged, so the T06 fields are required too, and a test
   compares every type with its handler.
8. **`/admin` is wrapped in `RequireRole`**, so tier1 and tier2 are turned away from it directly and a visitor reaches `/login` carrying
   `/admin`.
9. **Wording.** `admin.user_updated` is labelled "Cập nhật người dùng", not the canvas sample's "Đổi vai trò người dùng", because the event also
   covers a deactivation, a reset and an unlock. The reason texts drop the canvas's design annotations ("(409)") and its example ids.
   `strings.ts` imports types from `api/admin.ts` (`import type`, erased).
10. **Commit trailers.** Each message ends with the two attribution lines of the session (`Co-Authored-By: Claude Sonnet 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`
